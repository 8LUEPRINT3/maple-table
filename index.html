Make it work

• Move daily — walk, snowshoe, skate, whatever gets you moving (8–10k steps).
• Lift heavy 3–4×/week if you're building muscle — diet without resistance training just shrinks you.
• Sleep 7–9 h — muscle builds and fat burns while you sleep.
• Drink water (~3 L/day) — dehydration is the silent killer of energy and focus.
